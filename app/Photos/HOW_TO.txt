OUR OWN PHOTOS FOR THE APP
==========================
Put the photos in this folder (app/photos).
The name of the file starts with the name of the place or of the town, as it appears in the app.
After the name you can add " — ", ", " or " (" and any text.
Examples:
    Monsanto.jpg
    Óbidos.jfif
    Porto — Ribeira.jfif
    Santa Cruz das Flores, Portugal.jfif
    Lisbon.jfif  (Lisboa also works with the English name)
Formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .jfif, .png or .webp
The app uses our photo first. If there is no photo with that name, it looks for one on the internet.
New photos appear in the app without restarting it (refresh the page).
